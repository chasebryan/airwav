# MAX-I — Maximum Interpretation

MAX-I is not active in this capture foundation. The terminal truthfully displays MANUAL WINDOW and diagnostics report that scheduling is not enabled. No arbitrary scores or interpretation-yield percentages are generated.

After stable capture and one validated decoder, model window scores as separately logged components: measured activity, successful verified interpretation count, attempts, novelty, uncertainty, elapsed revisit time, burst timing, quality and exploration budget. Keep components separately inspectable and weights configurable.

EXPLORE, EXPLOIT, FOLLOW and VERIFY must have tested transitions and an explicit exploration budget to prevent starvation. Retunes require a new receiver-configuration epoch, settling exclusions, gap markers and fresh DSP/ring state. Each decision must preserve its inputs, candidate scores and reason. A single V4 cannot simultaneously observe 978 MHz, 1090 MHz and VHF aviation channels.
